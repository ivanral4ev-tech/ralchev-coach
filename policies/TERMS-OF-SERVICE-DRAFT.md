Information provided by the app before a coaching relationship begins should not be considered a complete individualized training program unless explicitly confirmed by Ivan Ralchev.

Ralchev Coach currently offers:

- 4-week Individual Training Plan — €30

The 4-week Individual Training Plan provides an individualized four-week training plan following assessment. It does not include ongoing weekly monitoring or continuous adjustments unless separately agreed.

Online Coaching includes individualized training planning, monitoring, feedback and adjustments, with direct communication with Ivan Ralchev.

Final service scope, availability and payment arrangements are confirmed directly with Ivan Ralchev before paid coaching begins.

## Safety and responsibility

Athletes are responsible for performing training in an appropriate and safe environment and for using suitable facilities and equipment.

Users should stop training and seek appropriate professional assistance if they experience symptoms or physical problems that may make continued training unsafe.
