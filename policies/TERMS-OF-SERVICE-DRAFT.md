# Ralchev Coach Terms of Service — draft

Ralchev Coach provides track & field coaching and training-planning services led by professional coach Ivan Ralchev.

## AI-assisted intake
AI may assist with initial information collection, organization and a general high-level assessment. AI is not presented as Ivan Ralchev and does not replace the coach. Individualized training plans and coaching decisions are made by Ivan Ralchev.

## Services and prices

- Online Coaching — €80/month.

Service scope and payment arrangements should be confirmed directly with Ivan Ralchev before paid coaching begins.

## No guarantee of results
Athletic performance depends on many factors. Ralchev Coach does not guarantee personal bests, qualification standards, medals, selection or other competitive outcomes.

## Medical limitation
Ralchev Coach is not a medical service and does not diagnose or treat injuries or medical conditions. Users with injuries, acute symptoms or medical concerns should seek qualified medical advice when appropriate.

## Minors
Paid individualized coaching for athletes under 18 should involve a parent or legal guardian.
