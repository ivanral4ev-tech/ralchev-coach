# Ralchev Coach Privacy Policy — draft

Ralchev Coach uses an AI-assisted intake workflow to help athletes understand whether professional track & field coaching by Ivan Ralchev may be suitable for them.

## Data requested
The service may ask for training-related information such as age, sex, height, weight, event, personal bests, goals, training experience, training availability, competition timing, available facilities and training limitations.

The intake does not require an athlete's full address, government identification, payment card information or medical records.

## Purpose
Training information is used only to provide an initial high-level assessment, explain Ralchev Coach services and help determine whether a four-week plan or ongoing online coaching may be appropriate.

## Health information
Ralchev Coach is not a medical service. Users should not submit detailed medical records. Any injury or limitation disclosed through intake is treated only as training context.

## Minors
For users under 18, the service provides only a general assessment and does not request direct contact details. A parent or legal guardian should participate before paid individualized coaching begins.

## External contact
Users who choose to continue may contact Ivan Ralchev through Instagram at @ralchev_coach. Instagram is a third-party service governed by its own privacy terms.
