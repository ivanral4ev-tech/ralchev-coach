# Ralchev Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Ralchev Coach provides an AI-assisted intake and assessment service for athletes interested in professional track and field coaching by Ivan Ralchev.

## Information requested

The service may ask users to provide training-related information such as age, sex, height, weight, athletics event, personal bests, training goals, training experience, weekly training availability, competition schedule, available facilities or equipment, and injuries or physical limitations relevant to training.

The service does not require a user's home address, government identification, payment card information, passwords, or detailed medical records.

## How information is used

Information provided through the Ralchev Coach ChatGPT app is used to provide an initial high-level athletics assessment, explain available Ralchev Coach services, and help determine whether a four-week individual training plan or ongoing online coaching may be appropriate.

The AI-assisted service does not independently create or provide paid individualized coaching programs on behalf of Ivan Ralchev. Individual training programs and coaching decisions are made by Ivan Ralchev.

## Data storage

The Ralchev Coach MCP service does not intentionally create user accounts or maintain its own database of athlete intake information.

Information submitted through ChatGPT may be processed and retained by OpenAI according to OpenAI's applicable privacy policies and user settings.

## Health information

Ralchev Coach is not a medical service and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Users should not submit detailed medical records. Information about an injury or physical limitation is used only as training context.

## Minors

For users under 18, the service provides only a general initial assessment and does not request direct contact details from the minor. A parent or legal guardian should participate before paid individualized coaching begins.

## Third-party services

Users who choose to continue with Ralchev Coach may contact Ivan Ralchev through Instagram at @ralchev_coach. Instagram and other third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Payments

The Ralchev Coach ChatGPT app does not currently process payments or collect payment card information.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Ralchev Coach service, its data practices, or applicable requirements change.
